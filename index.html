Common problems we solve

Project Difficulties

BuildMetrick helps you prevent the most common construction setbacks.

Delays

Delays occur when planned tasks or milestones take longer than expected, often due to unforeseen circumstances.

Improvisation

Improvisation refers to making quick, creative decisions or adjustments when unexpected challenges arise.

Missing Materials

Missing materials happen when essential supplies or equipment are not available at the right time.

Budget Out of Control

A budget out of control means project spending has exceeded planned limits, often due to poor cost estimation.
